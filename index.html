Active Learning, Active Life

Dixie State University is a public institution that was founded in 1911. It has a total of undergraduate enrollment of 8,570. Its setting is urban, and the campus size is 117 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.

  • Student-faculty ration - 20:1
  • Fall 2018 acceptance rate - 100%
  • Classes with fewer than 20 students - 38.4%
  • Average freshman retention rate - 57%
  • Accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities
